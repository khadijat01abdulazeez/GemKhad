## 👩🏽‍🔧 About Me

> *As a Mechanical Engineer managing plant reliability, my work centered on the physical reality of heavy industry—protecting high-stakes rotating equipment from premature wear, heat, and vibration. To shift from reactive fixes to proactive precision, I turned to data science. Currently, I combine SAP PM, SQL, Python, and Power BI to track material consumption, trim operational costs, and engineer predictive models that anticipate mechanical failures before they disrupt operations. Machines aren't the only systems that produce vital data signals—our ecosystems do too. My focus is on scaling these analytical tools into Environmental Data Science to track, predict, and eliminate industrial air and water pollution across the oil and gas value chain"*
